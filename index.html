CUL — Culture

Understanding your business ethos. We take the time to learn how your organisation thinks, operates and connects with its audience — then build software that fits.

CHAR — The variable

In programming, char is a fundamental variable type — the building block of every string, every message, every interaction. It stands for our focus on getting the foundations right.

Our name is a deliberate fusion. “Culture” speaks to our dual commitment: understanding the unique ethos of every client’s business, and nurturing a cohesive, close-knit team culture of our own.

“Char” is a nod to the fundamentals of programming. Just as the char is the smallest meaningful unit in code, we believe exceptional software is built from meticulous attention to the details that matter most.

We work with shared values and modern development techniques, committed to delivering work we’re proud to stand behind.

How a project runs

Six steps, with your sign-off before each big move.

  1. Discovery

    A requirements session, then a sitemap, feature list and written scope.

  2. Proposal

    Fixed scope and price in a signed proposal. A deposit starts the work.

  3. Design

    Your brand applied to CulChar layouts, with two rounds of revisions.

  4. Build

    Development on a private staging link so you can see progress and sign it off.

  5. Test & launch

    Functional, mobile and browser testing, then domain, SSL and go-live.

  6. Handover & care

    A training session and admin guide, then a care plan keeps it secure.
